Admissions

Parents apply online for Classes 6, 7, 8 or 9 through the OAVET form on the BSE Odisha portal. The application window is 27 October to 16 November 2026 (midnight).

Open bseodisha.ac.in

  1. Visit the portal. Open the official OAVS or BSE Odisha website and find the OAVET Application Form link.
  2. Register. Choose “New Registration for Parents,” enter the parent’s mobile number, create a password, select the class (6, 7, 8 or 9) and confirm with the OTP.
  3. Log in with your mobile number and password.
  4. Fill in details. Enter the student’s name, parents’ names, date of birth, category, gender, and Aadhaar numbers of the student and parents.
  5. Add your address and school. Enter permanent and present addresses. The OAV you can apply to is assigned from your permanent block and district.
  6. Upload documents (listed below).
  7. Review and final submit. Check every detail, then click Final Submit.
  8. Print the acknowledgment. Keep the application form and receipt for future reference.

Documents to upload

  • Student and parent Aadhaar cards
  • Passport-size photograph and student signature
  • Parent or guardian signature
  • Residence certificate
  • Category certificate (SC/ST/SEBC), if applicable
  • PwD certificate, if applicable
